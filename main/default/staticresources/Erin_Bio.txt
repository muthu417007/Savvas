Erin’s role at Savvas includes work with vendor and customer contracts as well as administration of Savvas’ insurance portfolio.  Prior to joining Savvas, Erin worked as outside defense counsel for real estate, transportation, and small business clients.  Erin is a “second career” attorney; before law school she worked for insurance companies for 16 years in various roles, including public affairs, underwriting, claims, and sales.  
Away from the office, Erin has a passion for motorcycles and autos, having spent summers in her youth with her cousin’s Formula Ford racing team and travelling to car shows.  Erin is always happy to discuss Formula One racing and “real” football, known in the U.S. as soccer.  She also loves to spend time at the gym every day and hiking Arizona’s trails, just not in the summer.  In law school, Erin developed an avid interest in international human rights and civil rights, authoring law journal articles on potential reforms of criminal and employment law using international legal norms.  
Erin is available to assist you with your legal needs.  While she focuses on contracts and insurance issues, Erin is happy to help however she can, and can direct you to additional resources if needed.
