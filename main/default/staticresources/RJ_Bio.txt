Ryan is a certified data privacy attorney, former IT engineer and published author with over 20 years' experience with enterprise technology. In his current roles, Ryan serves as Savvas' data privacy counsel and chief privacy officer responsible for providing day-to-day legal, contractual and regulatory counsel on data privacy matters as well as establishing and leading Savvas' Data Privacy Office (DPO). The DPO is a component of Savvas’ overall Data Privacy and Security program (DP&S) (LINK to DP&S page) that manages company compliance, Information Security and training.
