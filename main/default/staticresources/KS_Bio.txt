Kevin is a longtime transactional attorney, having spent his 26-plus years of legal practice primarily in in-house roles, including over 21 years at Pearson and now Savvas.  In his current role as Savvas’ VP & Senior Counsel, Kevin manages many of the company’s agreements with both our suppliers and our customers, and lends support to various initiatives of the company such as potential acquisitions or strategic partnerships.  As a result of his long history in the K-12 industry, Kevin is well-versed in the unique challenges Savvas faces with our public entity customers, such as strict bidding requirements, most-favored-nation statutory considerations, and an increasingly complex data privacy framework.  
Although Kevin has been an attorney for his entire adult working life, he prides himself on never having stepped into a courtroom at any time in his career.  He has simple tastes – he thinks McDonald’s is a great restaurant – and a fairly mundane list of things he enjoys, such as spending time with his family and watching Arizona sports teams (lose, mostly).  A few of his interests are more atypical, such as game theory.  But his primary hobby outside of work is Scrabble, and we’re not talking about the friendly kitchen-table game you might play when you get together over Thanksgiving, but rather a highly competitive fringe of word nerds who play in tournaments with timed games and memorize word lists in their free time.  At his peak Kevin was the 5th highest rated Scrabble player in Arizona, and he takes more satisfaction in that than anything he has ever accomplished as a lawyer. 
As with all members of the Savvas legal team, Kevin is here to help.  Please feel free to contact him about any company-related matter for which you need legal assistance, and if he doesn’t know the answer, he will help you get to someone who does.
