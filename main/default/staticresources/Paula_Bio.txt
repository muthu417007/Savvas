As part of the Savvas legal team, Paula helps oversee the Savvas’s intellectual property portfolio, including acquiring, registering and protecting the Company’s trademarks, working with authors and developers, and assisting product development, rights and permissions, procurement and the royalty department teams as needed on related rights and royalty issues. 
Paula’s experience in the publishing industry includes working in trade, higher education and the PreK to 12 education field, with her journey in the industry having begun as a production and manufacturing buyer with Hayden Book Company, then moving into positions with greater responsibility with Prentice Hall, Simon & Schuster and Pearson Education, and along the way eventually moving into the publishing legal arena after receiving her Juris Doctor from Seton Hall University School of Law.  In addition, Paula received a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology and a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from William Paterson University along with taking several publishing courses at NYU.
