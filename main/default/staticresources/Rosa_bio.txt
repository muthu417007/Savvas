Rosa Balestrino (Paramus, NJ – remote)

As VP & General Attorney, Rosa manages agreements with suppliers, and lends support to various initiatives of Savvas such as potential acquisitions or strategic partnerships. She works closely with Kevin Schutz and reports to Andy Yoo. Early in her career, she worked as a management consultant in Italy where she has extended family. She enjoys cooking and baking for family and friends, foreign travel, and exploring different cultures and cuisines (Indian cooking classes).
