As senior vice president and general counsel for Savvas, Andy leads the legal team to collaborate with and enable the Savvas business units on all legal matters, including commercial contracts, intellectual property, data privacy, compliance, real estate, and labor/employment. He is also responsible for assessing the impact of new laws and regulations on the company’s product offerings and operations. Andy comes to Savvas from Pearson Education, where he served as vice president and associate general counsel. Andy has more than 20 years of legal experience working across the education sector, from PreK-12 and higher education to professional and career development/employability. Andy received a Bachelor of Arts in economics from Cornell University and a Juris Doctor (Doctor of Law) from New York University School of Law.
Throughout his career, Andy has been an advocate and champion for greater diversity and inclusion within the legal profession.   He is on the board of the Asian American Bar Association of New York and served on the boards of the Pipeline to Practice Foundation and the Minority Corporation Counsel Association.   He enjoys live music (particularly jazz) and anything to do with the outdoors and nature (hiking, biking, skiing).
